              How advisories are shown

              Only official, active advisories that were verified for the area show in a red box, with who issued them. Heads-up items are not advisories. If a source couldn't be reached, the report says so instead of showing nothing.

              "None verified" means none was found in the sources checked. It does not mean conditions are workable or the water is clean.

              Glossary

              Hawaiian scale
              The local way of calling wave size. It usually reads smaller than the face; the face is often about twice the number.
              Canoes vs regional
              Canoes sizes are break-specific estimates. The regional South Shore forecast covers exposed south-facing breaks and is much bigger. One never stands in for the other.
              Offshore swell
              Swell height measured or forecast out at sea. It is not the height of breaking waves at any break.
              Swell period
              Seconds between waves. Long periods (14 s and up) carry more energy, so sets push harder and can drive current even when waves look small.
              Set penetration
              How far bigger set waves reach into the inside teaching zone.
              Teaching lane
              The patch of water you work in with a student. Losing it to drift or whitewater is a no-go signal.
              Rightward drift
              The along-shore pull that tends to follow sets at Canoes.
              Verified
              Confirmed from an official source this run.
              Observed
              Measured by an instrument, such as a buoy.
              Forecast
              A prediction from a weather or ocean model.
              From brief
              Stated in the brief without a specific source label.
              Inferred
              A reasoned estimate, not a measurement. Current is always inferred unless measured.
              Not verified
              The source couldn't confirm it either way.
              Unavailable
              The value couldn't be obtained. It is left blank, never guessed.
              GO, CONDITIONAL, NO-GO
              The beginner lesson call. Conditional means workable only if what you see on the beach checks out.
              Best, Conditional, Avoid windows
              Times of day ranked for beginner lessons, each with its reasons.

              How the report decides

              The beginner call

              GO

              Conditions point to a normal beginner lesson in the best window.

              CONDITIONAL

              Workable only if the on-beach checks pass. The report lists them.

              NO-GO

              A hazard or conditions point to not running beginner lessons.

              Two separate ratings

              General surf quality and beginner lesson suitability are rated separately. Small, poor-quality Canoes can be a good teaching day, and a great swell for Town can be a hard day for beginners.

              What it never does

              It never maps the regional forecast or offshore swell onto Canoes. It never calls long-period 1–2 ft surf easy by default. It never says "safe", never says the water is clean, and never claims there are no Oʻahu or statewide water advisories unless the full DOH list was read. It never invents a value when a source is down.

              When it updates

              A full refresh runs every day at about 6:30 AM and 6:30 PM HST. The morning run rates today. The evening run rates tomorrow, with lower confidence. After 14 hours without an update the report is marked stale.

              Who decides

              You do. Your on-site judgment overrides the report every time.
